# Domain Context

## Glossary

### Draft Revision

A platform-specific identity for the publishable parts of a local draft. The WeChat revision includes the article, inserted images, and default digest; the Xiaoheihe revision also includes its community and topics.

### Delivery

A successful transfer of one draft revision to a platform-owned draft or editor. A WeChat draft sync and a Xiaoheihe editor fill are separate deliveries.

### Publication Confirmation

A user's acknowledgement that a specific delivery on one platform was manually published. Confirmation on one platform never confirms another platform.

### Stale Confirmation

A historical publication acknowledgement whose draft revision, delivery, or remote payload is no longer the current one. It remains auditable but cannot authorize the current draft as published.
